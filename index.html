Are collection colours the same as garment colours?

No. A collection’s colour is part of its visual identity. Each product lists its own garment colourways separately.

Which collections can I shop right now?

Tiger × Black is the current collection represented in this preview. The other animal and colour combinations shown above are concept examples, not available releases.

Where can I check fit and size information?

Open a product to review its available sizes and product details. A verified size chart can be added when the final measurements are supplied.
